# Category O
**Prerequisites:** [[Representation Theory (Hub)]], [[Universal Enveloping Algebra]], [[Highest Weight Category (Hub)]]

## Definitions
Category O, introduced by Bernstein-Gelfand-Gelfand (BGG), is an important category of representations of finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebras. It focuses on modules that satisfy certain finiteness and highest-weight conditions.

## Properties
It provides a framework for classifying highest weight modules and serves as the prototype for highest weight categories.
