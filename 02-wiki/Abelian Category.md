# Abelian Category

An **abelian category** is a category that behaves like the category of abelian groups. It allows for the definition of kernels, cokernels, and exact sequences.

Key properties:
- It is additive.
- Every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel.
- Every monomorphism is the kernel of its cokernel.
- Every epimorphism is the cokernel of its kernel.
