# Semiperfect Ring

A ring $R$ is called **semiperfect** if every [[Simple Object]] (irreducible module) in the category of $R$-modules has a [[Projective Cover]].

## Properties
- In a semiperfect ring, every finite-dimensional module has a projective cover.
- Finite-dimensional algebras over a field are semiperfect.
