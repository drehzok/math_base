# Poincare Duality
**Prerequisites:** [[Cohomology (Hub)]], [[Homology (Hub)]]

## Theorems
Let $M$ be a compact $n$-dimensional manifold. Then there is an isomorphism:
$$ H^{n-i}(M; \mathbb{F}_2) \xrightarrow{\cong} H_i(M; \mathbb{F}_2) $$
given by the "cap-product" with a fundamental class $\mu_M \in H_n(M; \mathbb{F}_2)$.

### Corollary
If $M$ is connected, then $H^n(M; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H_0(M; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong \mathbb{F}_2$, and the map:
$$ H^i(M; \mathbb{F}_2) \times H^{n-i}(M; \mathbb{F}_2) \to \mathbb{F}_2 $$
given by $(x, y) \mapsto \phi(x \cup y)$ is a non-degenerate bilinear form.
