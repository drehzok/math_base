# Whitehead Theorem (Chain Complexes)
**Prerequisites:** [[Chain Complex]], [[Quasi-Isomorphism (Hub)]]

## Theorems
Let $\delta: C \to D$ be a quasi-isomorphism of chain complexes. If $C$ and $D$ are bounded below and degree-wise free (or more generally, projective), then $\delta$ is a chain homotopy equivalence.
