# Quasi-Isomorphism (Hub)
**Prerequisites:** [[Chain Complex]], [[Cohomology (Hub)]]

## Definitions
A quasi-isomorphism (often abbreviated as "gis" or "qis") is a morphism of chain complexes that induces an isomorphism on their homology/cohomology groups.
