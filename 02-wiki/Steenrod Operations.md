# Steenrod Operations

## Definition
For cohomology with $\mathbb{F}_2$ coefficients, there exist natural transformations called **Steenrod squares**:
$$Sq^i: H^n(X; \mathbb{F}_2) \to H^{n+i}(X; \mathbb{F}_2)$$
These operations provide more structure than the [[Cup Product]].

## Steenrod Algebra
Together, these operations assemble into a natural action by the **Steenrod algebra** $\mathcal{A}_2$ on $H^*(X; \mathbb{F}_2)$.
