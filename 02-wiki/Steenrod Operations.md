# Steenrod Operations
**Prerequisites:** [[Singular Cohomology]]

## Definitions
For cohomology with $\mathbb{F}_2$ coefficients, there exist natural transformations called **Steenrod squares**:
$$ Sq^i: H^n(X; \mathbb{F}_2) \to H^{n+i}(X; \mathbb{F}_2) $$
These operations assemble into a natural action by the Steenrod algebra $\mathcal{A}_2$ on $H^*(X; \mathbb{F}_2)$. This gives the cohomology ring more natural structure than just the cup product.
