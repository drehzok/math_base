# Quiver Representation
**Prerequisites:** [[Representation Theory (Hub)]], [[Category Theory]]

## Definitions
Let $\Gamma$ be a quiver (an oriented graph). The category of finite-dimensional representations of $\Gamma$ is equivalent to the category of functors from $\Gamma$ (viewed as a category) to $\operatorname{Vec}_k$, which is also equivalent to the category of modules over the path algebra $k\Gamma^{op}$.
