# Grothendieck Group
**Prerequisites:** [[Jordan-Holder Series]], [[Abelian Category]]

## Definitions
For an abelian category $\mathcal{A}$ where every object has finite length (e.g., admitting a Jordan-Holder series), the Grothendieck group $K_0(\mathcal{A})$ is the free abelian group generated by the isomorphism classes of simple objects. The class of a module $[M]$ corresponds to the sum of its composition factors with their multiplicities.
