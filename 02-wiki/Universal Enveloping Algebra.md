# Universal Enveloping Algebra
**Prerequisites:** [[Lie Algebra]]

## Definitions
The universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{g})$ of a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ is the most general unital associative algebra into which $\mathfrak{g}$ can be embedded such that the Lie bracket corresponds to the commutator. It is used extensively in defining modules, such as Verma modules in Category O.
