# Highest Weight Category
**Prerequisites:** [[Category O (Hub)]]

## Definitions
A highest weight category is an abelian category built from simple objects and projective covers in a structured way, often possessing homological properties similar to Category O. Many categories in Lie theory can be described as highest weight categories or derived from them.
