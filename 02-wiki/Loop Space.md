# Loop Space
**Prerequisites:** [[Mapping Space]]

## Definitions
The loop space of a pointed space $Y$, denoted $\Omega Y$, is defined as $\operatorname{map}_*(S^1, Y)$.

## Properties
Loop spaces are "nice" with respect to homotopy groups, as $\pi_n(\Omega Y, c_{y_0}) \cong \pi_{n+1}(Y, y_0)$, but they are "messy" with respect to homologies and cohomologies.
