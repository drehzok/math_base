# Jordan-Holder Series
**Prerequisites:** [[Simple Object]]

## Definitions
A Jordan-Holder series (or composition series) is a finite filtration of an object where the successive quotients are simple objects. The multiplicity of each simple quotient is unique, leading to a well-defined image in the Grothendieck group.
