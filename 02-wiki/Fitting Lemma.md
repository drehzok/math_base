# Fitting Lemma
**Prerequisites:** [[Bichain Condition]], [[Indecomposable Object]]

## Theorems
Let $X$ be an object in an abelian category $\mathcal{A}$ satisfying the bichain condition, and let $\phi \in \operatorname{End}_\mathcal{A}(X)$. Then:
$$ X = \operatorname{im}(\phi^r) \oplus \ker(\phi^r) $$
for some integer $r \gg 0$.

In particular, if $X$ is indecomposable, then any endomorphism $\phi$ is either nilpotent or an isomorphism.
