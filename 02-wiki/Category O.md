# Category O

**Category O** is a specific category of modules over a semisimple Lie algebra, introduced by Bernstein, Gelfand, and Gelfand (BGG). It serves as a prototype for **highest weight categories**.

## Motivation
Category O was introduced to study the representations of semisimple Lie algebras that are not necessarily finite-dimensional but share some of their nice properties, such as having a weight space decomposition.

## Basic Properties
- It is an [[Abelian Category]].
- Every object in Category O has finite length.
- It has enough [[Projective Object]]s.
- The simple objects are indexed by their highest weights.
